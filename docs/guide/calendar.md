# Calendars

Open **Calendar** in the menu. **My calendar** shows the school calendar and
the calendars of all your classes together. Use the menu at the top to see
just the school calendar, or one class.

- **Month** shows the whole month. On a phone, days with events have colored
  dots, and the events are listed below the month.
- **Week** and **List** show events day by day. List covers the next 60 days.
- Use **‹** and **›** to move back and forward, and **Today** to come back.

Days without school (holidays, breaks) are shaded and marked **No school**.
Assignment due dates show as **Due: …** with a dashed outline, and open the
assignment; they're on subscribed calendars too.
The home page shows what's coming up in the next two weeks. Each class's page
has a **Coming up** card with that class's events and due dates for the next
month, a link to its calendar, and **Add event** for its mentors.

## Subscribing in Google, Apple or Outlook calendars

Select **Subscribe** on the calendar page (or **Subscribe to this class** on a
class's page). You get a private link for your whole calendar, the school
calendar alone, and each of your classes. Every person's links are their own.

- **iPhone, iPad and Mac:** tap **Apple Calendar**, then Subscribe.
- **Google Calendar:** tap **Google Calendar**, or copy the link and, on a
  computer, go to *Other calendars → + → From URL*.
- **Outlook:** copy the link, then *Add calendar → Subscribe from web*.

The calendar app then keeps itself up to date: Apple checks about every hour,
Google and Outlook less often (up to a day). Google and Outlook need Taper to
have an `https://` address.

**The links are private:** anyone with one can see that calendar. If one gets
out, select **Reset this link** next to it: that link stops working and gets
a new address, and your other links keep working. **Reset all my links**
replaces every one. Subscribe again with the new address wherever you used the
old one. A class's link stops working if you leave the class.

## Adding events

- **Admins** add events to the school calendar, and to any class's calendar.
- **Mentors** add events to the calendars of the classes they mentor.

Select **Add event** (on the calendar, or on a class's page) and choose:

- **Calendar:** the school calendar or one of your classes.
- **Title**, and whether it's **all day** or has start and end times. An event
  can last several days (a trip, a break). Changing the start moves the end
  with it, keeping the same length; an end before the start is marked and
  can't be saved.
- **Repeats:** every day, every week on the days you pick (such as Tuesday and
  Thursday), every month on the same date, or every year. Add **Until** to stop
  it, such as at the end of the term.
- **Where** and **Details**, optionally. Details can have simple formatting.
- **No school** (school calendar only), for holidays and breaks.

### Changing and removing events

Open an event and select **Edit**. For a repeating event, a change applies to
every date. To cancel one date of a repeating event (for example, no class on a
holiday), open that date and select **Remove just this date**. **Delete**
removes the event, and all its dates if it repeats.

## Time zone

Events are in the school's local time, set by an admin under **Settings →
School → Time zone**. A class at 10:00 stays at 10:00 when daylight saving
time starts or ends.
