# Assignments

Mentors set assignments for their classes. Scholars read them, do the work —
in Taper, as files, or on paper — and turn it in. Mentors look at the work and
mark it **Complete** or ask for **another look**, with written feedback. There
are no grades.

Each class's page has an **Assignments** card with what's coming up, and
**All** to see every assignment in the class. The **Assignments** card on your
home page brings together what needs you across all your classes.

## For scholars

The **Assignments** card on your home page lists everything you haven't turned
in yet, and work your mentor asked you to look at again, from all your classes,
soonest due first. Work still not turned in after its due time says
**Past due**.

A class's assignments (**All** on the class's Assignments card) show where each
one stands: Not turned in, Turned in (waiting for your mentor), Needs another
look, or Complete.

### Doing the work

Open an assignment to read the instructions and download any files your mentor
attached. Then, depending on the assignment:

- **Write in Taper:** type in the box. It saves by itself a few seconds after
  you stop typing ("Saved at 3:04 PM"), so you can close the page and come back
  later, on any device.
- **Attach files:** choose one or more files (up to 25 MB each): a document,
  photos of handwritten work, a recording. Select **Save without turning in**
  to keep them without turning in yet, or remove one with **Remove**.
- **On paper or in person:** there's nothing to write or attach; just select
  **Turn in** when you've done it.

When you're ready, select **Turn in**. Your mentor sees it straight away.

Changed your mind? **Take it back to change it**, as long as your mentor hasn't
looked at it yet. Then turn it in again.

### Feedback

When your mentor has looked at your work, the assignment shows their feedback
and one of:

- **Complete:** well done, nothing more to do.
- **Needs another look:** read the feedback, change your work, and select
  **Turn in again**.

**History** at the bottom shows when the work was turned in, taken back,
sent back and marked complete.

## For mentors

### Setting an assignment

On the class's page, select **New assignment** in the Assignments card. Fill in:

- **Title**, and **instructions** with simple formatting: `**bold**`,
  `*italic*`, lists with `- `, links with `[text](https://…)`.
- **Files** to attach: readings, worksheets, templates (up to 25 MB each).
- **Due** date and, optionally, a time. Without a time, it's due by the end of
  the day. Due dates show on the class calendar, everyone's **My calendar**,
  and in [calendar subscriptions](calendar.md#subscribing-in-google-apple-or-outlook-calendars).
- **How scholars turn it in:** write in Taper, attach files, both, or neither
  (for work done on paper or in person).
- **When scholars see it:** **now**, **later** from a day and time (it appears
  by itself then), or **not yet**, as a draft only mentors can see.

Edit an assignment at any time, including adding or removing files. Deleting
an assignment also deletes the work turned in for it.

### Looking at work

The **Assignments** card on your home page lists assignments in your classes
with work waiting for you ("2 to review") first, then what's due in the next
two weeks. Each class's page lists all its assignments.

On an assignment's page, **Scholars' work** lists every scholar and where their
work is: Not turned in, Turned in, Needs another look, or Complete, and when
they turned it in (marked **late** if after the due time). Select **Review**.

On a scholar's work:

1. Read what they wrote and open their files.
2. Write **feedback**: what went well, and what to think about next.
3. Select **Mark complete**, or **Needs another look** (which needs feedback,
   so they know what to change).

Taper then takes you to the next piece of work waiting, if there is one. Use
the arrows at the top to move between scholars.

You can also mark work complete that wasn't turned in through Taper, such as
something done in class.

## Who sees what

- Scholars see their classes' published assignments and only **their own**
  work and feedback.
- A class's mentors and admins see every scholar's work in it.
- Other mentors can see a class's published assignments, but not scholars' work.
- Files can only be downloaded by people who can see the assignment or work
  they're attached to.

When a class is archived, its assignments and work are kept, read-only.
