# Notifications

Taper tells you about things that concern you. The **bell** at the top right
shows how many are new. Select it to see them, and select one to open what it's
about.

## What you're told about

| Kind | Who hears |
|------|-----------|
| **New assignments** | Scholars in the class, and their parents, when an assignment is published (straight away, or at the time a mentor chose). |
| **Work and feedback** | Mentors when work is turned in for them to review. Scholars and their parents when work needs another look, is marked complete, or is returned. A scholar also hears when a parent turns work in for them. |
| **Due tomorrow** | Scholars and their parents, from 3 PM the afternoon before work is due, if it isn't turned in yet. |
| **Chat messages** | Everyone in the chat. Community messages go to everyone. Several messages in one chat are combined, like "3 new messages in Latin I", until you open it. |
| **Calendar** | New, changed and cancelled events: school events for everyone, and class events for the class and its scholars' parents. |

You're never told about something you did yourself. Several pieces of work
turned in for one assignment are combined too, until the mentor looks.

Notifications are kept for 60 days.

## Choosing what you're told

Open **My account** (select your initials) and scroll to **Notifications**.
Untick the kinds you don't want, and select **Save notifications**.

## By email

Email is off until you turn it on. To turn it on:

1. Add your email address under **Profile** on My account.
2. Under **Notifications → Email**, choose:
   - **An email for each one**, sent as it happens. A chat sends one email until
     you read it, not one for every message.
   - **A summary each evening**, from 6 PM, listing what's new, and only when
     there is something.
3. Select **Save notifications**.

Emails say what happened and link to it. They don't include what anyone wrote,
like messages or feedback; open Taper to read those. Emails only go out once
an admin has set up [email](email.md) for the school.

Notifications on your phone's lock screen (web push) are coming in a later
version.
