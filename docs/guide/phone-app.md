# Taper on your phone

Taper can be added to your phone's or tablet's home screen. It then opens in its
own window like any other app, with the school's candle icon. There's nothing to
download from an app store.

> Adding Taper to the home screen needs your school's Taper to use a secure
> address, one that starts with `https://`. If you don't see the option, ask your
> school's admin (see [Installing Taper](install.md#https)).

## iPhone and iPad

1. Open Taper in **Safari** and sign in.
2. Tap the **Share** button (a square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add**.

On iOS 16.4 and later this also works from Chrome and Edge, through their own
share menus.

## Android

1. Open Taper in **Chrome** and sign in.
2. Tap the **⋮** menu at the top right.
3. Tap **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**, then **Install**.

Samsung Internet, Edge and Firefox have the same option in their menus.

## Computers

Chrome and Edge can install Taper as an app too. Look for the install icon at the
right end of the address bar.

## Without a connection

Taper needs an internet connection. If you open it while offline, it says so,
and has a **Try again** button.
