# Your account

Select your name at the top of any page to open **My account**.

## Profile

You can change your **name**, which is how it appears to others in Taper, and
your **email address**. Email is optional. Only the people who manage accounts
at your school can see it.

Your **username** and **role** can only be changed by an admin.

## Changing your password

1. Under **Password**, type your current password.
2. Type the new password twice. Use at least 8 characters.
3. Select **Change password**.

You stay signed in on the device you're using. Any other phones, tablets or
computers you were signed in on are signed out.

## Where you're signed in

This list shows every browser where you're signed in, and when each was last
used. If you see one you don't recognize, or you forgot to sign out on a shared
computer, select **Sign out everywhere else**. If you think someone knows your
password, change it as well.
