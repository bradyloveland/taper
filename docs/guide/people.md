# Managing people

*For admins.* Open **People** in the menu.

## Adding someone

1. Select **Add a person**.
2. Enter their **name**. Taper suggests a **username** from it (like `ann.adams`).
   You can change it. Usernames are 2 to 40 letters, numbers, dots, dashes or
   underscores.
3. Add an **email** if you like. It's optional, and scholars don't need one.
4. Choose a **role**: Scholar, Mentor or Admin.
5. Select **Add and create a temporary password**.

Taper shows the web address, the username and a **temporary password**. Give
these to the person on paper (**Print**), in person, or however your school
shares such things. The password is shown **only once**. When they first sign
in, they choose their own.

The People list marks anyone who hasn't chosen their own password yet with
**Temporary password**.

## Importing from a spreadsheet

To add many people at once, select **Import from a spreadsheet** on the People
page.

1. Make a spreadsheet whose first row names the columns:
   - **Name** (or **First name** and **Last name**): required.
   - **Username:** optional. Taper makes one from the name if it's empty.
   - **Email:** optional.
   - **Role:** scholar, mentor or admin. Empty means scholar.
   - **Classes:** optional. Class names separated by `;`. The classes must already
     exist. Scholars join as scholars and mentors as mentors.
2. Save it as CSV (Excel, Numbers and Google Sheets all can), and choose the file,
   or copy the cells and paste them.
3. Select **Check the list**. Taper shows each person, and any row with a problem
   (a username that's taken, a class that doesn't exist…) in red. Problem rows are
   skipped; fix them and import them again later.
4. Select **Add**. Taper shows everyone's username and temporary password **once**.
   **Download the list** or **Print** it before leaving the page.

## Finding people

Search by name, username or email, and filter by role. **Status** shows active
accounts by default; choose **Deactivated** or **Everyone** to see the others.

## Changing someone's details or role

Select their name, change what you need, and select **Save changes**.

- You can't change your own role. Another admin can.
- The school always needs at least one admin. To change the last admin's role,
  make someone else an admin first.

## Resetting a password

If someone forgets their password, open their page and select **Reset
password**. Their old password stops working, and they're signed out on every
device. Give them the new temporary password.

To change your own password, use [My account](account.md) instead.

## When someone leaves

Open their page, turn off **Can sign in**, and save. They're signed out
everywhere and can't sign in, but their work and messages are kept. Turn it back
on at any time.

Accounts are deactivated rather than deleted, so classes and assignments keep a
full record.

## A person's classes

Each person's page lists the classes they're in. To change that, use the class's
**Members** page (see [Classes](classes.md)).
