# Taper guide

*Light your taper at mine.*

Taper is your school's place for classes, calendars, assignments and chat. This
guide is in the app (under **Guide**) and on GitHub.

## For everyone

- [Getting started](getting-started.md): signing in for the first time, and what admins, mentors and scholars can do.
- [Classes](classes.md): your classes, and how admins and mentors set them up.
- [Your account](account.md): your name, password, and signing out on other devices.
- [Two-step sign-in](two-step.md): add a code from your phone, and what to do if you lose it.
- [Taper on your phone](phone-app.md): add Taper to your home screen on iPhone, iPad and Android.
- [Reporting a problem](reporting-problems.md): tell the people who make Taper when something's wrong.

## For admins

- [Managing people](people.md): add mentors and scholars, reset passwords, and deactivate accounts.
- [Network and HTTPS](network.md): how people reach Taper, HTTPS with Let's Encrypt, reverse proxies.
- [Email](email.md): send password reset links and notices to admins.
- [Updates and backups](updates.md): install new versions from the web interface, go back if needed, and back up.
- [Installing Taper](install.md): put Taper on a Debian server, set up HTTPS, and upgrade.
- [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md): locked out, can't reach the server, and where the logs are.
