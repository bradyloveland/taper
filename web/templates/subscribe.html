How to add it

  • iPhone, iPad and Mac: tap Apple Calendar, then Subscribe.
  • Google Calendar: tap Google Calendar, or copy the link and, on a computer, go to Other calendars → + → From URL.
  • Outlook: copy the link, then Add calendar → Subscribe from web.

Calendar apps check for changes on their own schedule: Apple every hour or so, Google and Outlook less often (up to a day).
