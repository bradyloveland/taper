How passkeys work

  • Your phone or computer keeps the passkey, and asks for your fingerprint, face or PIN each time you use it. It counts as two-step sign-in.
  • On an iPhone, iPad or Mac, passkeys sync through iCloud Keychain; on Android, through your Google account. A passkey made on one of your devices usually works on the others. Those show Synced.
  • To sign in, select Sign in with a passkey on the sign-in page.
  • Your password still works{{if .D.Required}}, together with a code,{{end}} if you lose your devices. If a device is lost or stolen, remove its passkey here.
{{end}}
